Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Restaurants and food-service businesses have hot water demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Dish machines, three-compartment sinks, prep sinks, mop sinks, and handwashing stations all draw at once during a rush, and a tank with too little recovery falls behind right when the dish room backs up. Flint Water Heater handles food-service hot water, including high-input gas tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters for high-temperature dish machines, proper sizing based on peak-hour demand, and scheduled service built around your hours.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to provide safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide from a blocked or undersized vent, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Flint Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A commercial water heater failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens can't sanitize, salons turn clients away, hotel guests complain, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets an experienced technician dispatched quickly for emergency repairs anywhere in Flint and the surrounding area, and scheduled work can be done overnight or before opening to limit downtime.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. The cause could be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit throwing error codes. Whatever it is, we find it fast, restore hot water, and document the entire call for your facility files. Then we put maintenance in place so the same failure doesn't return.

Ongoing Protection

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Flint Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and verification of delivered water temperature.

Compared with an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties that stay on a schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan sized to your property and budget.
